## 🌟 Key Features & Systems

### ⚔️ Rich Action RPG Combat System
- **Light & Heavy Attacks**: Left Click (`LMB`) for quick combos, or hold for heavy finisher strikes.
- **Parry & Dodge Roll**: Right Click (`RMB`) to parry and stagger enemies with precise timing; `Shift` for a smooth dodge roll.
- **Special Boss Push Attack (`U`)**: A legendary frog jump attack dealing devastating area-of-effect damage and knocking back enemies.
- **Target Lock**: `F` or `Middle Mouse Button` to lock onto targets.
- **Weapon & Equipment Loadouts**: Hammers, swords, scythes, magic staves, rings, and ancient armor sets.

### 🌲 Open World & "The Lands Between" Ecosystem
- **Ancient Golden Tree of Life (Erdtree)**: A colossal 180-meter golden tree rising into the northern horizon alongside descending pillars of light.
- **Sites of Grace**: Golden rest shrines scattered across the world that fully restore the player's health and stamina.
- **Golden Leaf Storm**: Dynamic weather featuring golden glowing leaves swirling in the wind.
- **Living World**: Flocks of birds gliding across the sky, butterflies and fireflies fluttering among flowers, and fleeing woodland fauna (rabbits, deer).

### 👁️ God's Eye in the Sky & Lore Shrines
- **Real-Time Gaze Tracking**: The divine eye in the sky tracks player movements step-by-step across the entire map.
- **Rotating Ophanim Rings & God Rays**: Divine rings and shafts of light descending to the earth.
- **5 Lore Shrines & Graveyard of Champions**: Interactive inscriptions (`X`) revealing deep lore and the histories of ancient champions.

### 🏰 4 Mega-Castles & Dungeons
- **Boss Fog Gates**, inner courtyards, elevators, and throne rooms.
- Hidden caves, subterranean catacombs, and cave bosses.

### 🌌 Interdimensional White Void & "The Whisperer" (God's Shadow)
- A white dimensional portal opening from the Ancient Eye Statue in the north.
- An 11-meter obsidian titan with a porcelain mask and laser eyes.
- Epic orchestral battle theme *"Legionnaire"* by Scott Buckley, backed by an integrated Web Audio synthesizer engine.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Movement |
| **Mouse** | Camera Angle |
| **Left Click (LMB)** | Light Attack *(Hold for Heavy Attack)* |
| **Right Click (RMB)** | Parry |
| **Shift** | Dodge Roll |
| **Space** | Jump |
| **U** | Special Boss Push / Slam Attack |
| **Q** | Summon / Dismount Spectral Steed |
| **F / Middle Mouse Click** | Target Lock |
| **TAB** | Inventory & Equipment Menu |
| **K** | Skill Tree |
| **M** | World Map |
| **X** | Interact with Lore Shrines & Tombstones |
| **ESC / P** | Pause Menu |
